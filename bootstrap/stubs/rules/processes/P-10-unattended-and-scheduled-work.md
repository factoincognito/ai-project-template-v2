# Process P-10. Unattended and scheduled work

P-10 § 1 Unattended work
(1) Given a scoped general instruction, a session works inside the stated scope only, through the normal change, review and merge flow.
(2) Anything that needs the product owner (a decision, an intent approval, a review escalated after three rounds, a question under § 8 (4), a step that is irreversible and could reasonably go either way) is parked in the "Waiting for the product owner" section of the project's backlog or in its staging file, not guessed. Intent approval is never assumed. Work that does not depend on it carries on.
(3) Progress is posted as it happens, in PR comments and in the chat.
(4) When the product owner is back, the session reports what merged (verified in accordance with Process P-4 § 3), what is parked and why, and what is open.

P-10 § 2 Unattended batches
(1) An unattended batch takes only work that needs no decision from the product owner.

P-10 § 3 Creating a scheduled task
(1) A scheduled task a session creates shall run without stopping to ask the product owner for permission: the session never sets its approval setting to ask, and after creating it reports the setting it got.
(2) When a task did not get automatic approval, the session tells the product owner in the For you block to switch it to "Automatically approve" in the task's settings.
(3) A recurring task is set a few minutes past the hour, not on the full hour.

P-10 § 4 Deleting a scheduled task
(1) A one-shot scheduled task is deleted, without asking, once it has fired and its work is done.
(2) The session that created it deletes it while that session is still running; otherwise the session the task started deletes its own task before ending.
